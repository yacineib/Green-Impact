Off-grid desalination and cold storage

One panel of sunlight. Clean water and food that lasts.

Green Impact is a dual-function solar system that desalinates water and refrigerates food, built for remote and underserved communities the grid (and most existing solutions) never reach.

Partner with us See how it works

Two crises, one root cause.

Remote and underserved communities are asked to solve water and food security separately, with fixes that assume a grid connection they don't have.

01

Water scarcity, unevenly felt

Reliable access to clean water remains out of reach across many remote and underserved regions, forcing households to rely on distant or unsafe sources.

02

Food spoils before it's eaten

Without refrigeration, harvested and stored food breaks down quickly, turning scarcity into waste, and waste into lost income.

03

Built on fuel that runs out

Diesel generators and grid extensions are expensive, unreliable, and dependent on supply chains that rarely favor remote areas.

04

Existing fixes solve one thing

Bottled water delivery, standalone coolers, and rainwater systems each address a single symptom. None combine water and food security in one affordable system.

A dual-function system, one power source.

Green Impact pairs solar-powered desalination with refrigeration in a single, integrated unit, designed to be installed, run, and maintained by the communities it serves.

Solar captures the energy

Photovoltaic panels built for off-grid, remote installation power the entire system: no fuel, no grid connection required.

One input, two outputs

The same solar power runs an MVC (mechanical vapor compression) desalination unit and a refrigeration unit side by side.

Delivered with training

Local teams are trained to install, operate, and maintain their own system, built for long-term independence, not dependency.

What makes it defensible.

A unique combination of technology and relationships that's hard to copy quickly.

Patented dual-function tech

MVC desalination and solar refrigeration engineered into one system, not bolted together. That pairing is the core of our unique value proposition.

Partnerships from day one

Designed for the last mile
